STEP 4: AFTER SENDING CONNECTION REQUEST — ENGAGE WITH THEIR CONTENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

After sending the connection request to a person, do NOT close their profile yet. Do the following:

1. Scroll down to their "Posts" or "Activity" section on their profile.
2. Find their 2 most recent posts (that are not just reposts/reshares — prefer original posts with their own thoughts, photos, or business updates).
3. For each of the 2 posts:
   a. Click the "Like" button (thumbs up or heart — use the heart/love reaction if the post is personal or celebratory, thumbs up for business content).
   b. Write a thoughtful comment that is:
      - Relevant to their post content — do NOT mention our product or website in comments
      - A genuine question — this starts a conversation and shows you actually read it
      - 2-4 sentences maximum — keep it concise
      - Not generic — never write "Great post!" or "Thanks for sharing!" or "Agreed!"


COMMENT STRATEGY — Based on post type:

If they posted about a business milestone/achievement:
> Congrats on [specific milestone]! Curious — what was the biggest driver behind this growth? Would love to learn from your journey.

If they posted about a challenge/struggle in their industry:
> Really relatable post. We see this a lot with [their industry] businesses. Have you found any specific strategy that's helped you navigate this?

If they posted tips/advice for others:
> Solid advice, especially the point about [specific thing they mentioned]. How long did it take you to figure that out? Would be great to hear more.

If they posted about their team/culture:
> Love seeing businesses invest in their team. What's your go-to method for keeping the team aligned as you scale?

If they posted a product/service update:
> This looks great! What made you decide to [specific thing they launched/changed]? Always interesting to see how businesses in [their niche] evolve.

If they posted about industry news/trends:
> Interesting take. Do you think this will significantly impact [their niche] businesses in the next 12 months, or is it more of a slow shift?


COMMENT RULES:
- ALWAYS ask a question — this is the #1 rule. Questions get replies. Replies build relationships.
- NEVER mention AI Social Reputation, our product, or our website in comments
- NEVER copy-paste the same comment on multiple posts — each must be unique
- Keep tone friendly, professional, and genuinely curious
- Do NOT use emojis in comments (keeps it professional)
